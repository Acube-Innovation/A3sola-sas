# Getting started — for the owner

You have signed up, paid, and your workspace is ready. This is what to do on the first day,
in the order that matters.

---

## 1. Sign in

Your welcome email has a link to set your password. If it has expired, use **Forgot
password** — the link is valid for 24 hours.

You land on the **A3 Sola** workspace with four sections: **Solar CRM**, **Solar
Operations**, **Solar Projects**, and your billing.

---

## 2. The setup checklist — before anybody enters real work

Your workspace already knows how a Kerala rooftop solar business works: the pipeline, the
nineteen execution stages, the document pack, the subsidy scheme and the five-year O&M
model are all set up.

**Four things were left blank on purpose, because guessing them would have been worse.**

### a. Your ledger accounts — with your accountant

**A3 Sola Settings → Projects → Account Mappings.**

Every mapping is empty. A guessed mapping posts real money to the wrong account and is
painful to unwind. Sit with your accountant once and fill them in.

**Until this is done, no accounting entries are made.** The app records everything and
posts nothing. That is deliberate and it is safe.

### b. Your tariff — check it against a real bill

**Solar CRM → Electricity Tariff.**

The seeded KSEB slabs were correct when they were written. Tariffs change. Open a recent
bill from any customer and check the slab rates match. Savings calculations on every
proposal you issue depend on this.

### c. GST — in writing, from your CA

**A3 Sola Settings → Projects → GST.**

Solar EPC is a composite supply and the industry commonly applies a 70:30 valuation. The
sources disagree, and the correct treatment depends on how your contracts are written.

**Get your CA's answer in writing.** Then set the valuation rule, tick *GST treatment
confirmed*, and only then switch on accounting postings. The app will not let you post
until you do.

### d. Your DISCOM section and local details

**Solar CRM → DISCOM Section.** Add the sections you actually work in. Consumer records
validate against these.

---

## 3. Your logo and letterhead

**Settings → Letter Head.** Every proposal, agreement and invoice you generate carries it.
Do this before you send the first proposal, not after.

---

## 4. Invite your team

**Team & Seats** in the portal menu, or **A3 Sola Settings → Users**.

Invite by email. Each person gets a role that decides what they see:

| Role | Sees |
|---|---|
| Sales Executive | Leads, consumers, surveys, proposals |
| Survey Engineer | Surveys and designs |
| Operations Manager | Installations, stages, statutory documents |
| Site Engineer | Their work orders and evidence uploads |
| Accounts Executive | Billing, invoices, payments |
| Service Coordinator | O&M visits, tickets, warranty |
| Administrator | Everything, plus billing and seats |

**Costs and margins are hidden from operational roles.** Your site engineer sees what to
build, not what it cost you.

---

## 5. Seats and plans

Your plan includes a number of seats. A seat is a person who can sign in.

- **Need one more?** Team & Seats → Add a seat. You see the prorated cost before you
  confirm, and if your account is in good standing the seat is available immediately.
- **Someone left?** Disable them — that frees the seat. Do not delete them, or you lose
  the record of what they did.
- **Need a bigger plan?** Billing → Change plan. You see the exact proration before
  confirming, and you are told plainly if the change affects how you pay.

**Downgrading with too many users:** you will be asked to choose who to remove. The app
will not choose for you, and if you have not chosen by your renewal date you simply stay on
your current plan for another month. Your data is never made unreachable.

---

## 6. Billing and invoices

**Billing** in the portal menu: your plan, next billing date, amount, every invoice, and
your full account history in plain language.

**If a payment fails** — usually an expired card — nothing happens to your access. You get
an email. If it stays unpaid you will see a banner, and you will get a clear warning with
an exact date before anything is restricted. **Paying restores everything immediately**, and
nothing is ever deleted.

---

## 7. Where to get help

- **In the app** — every field has a description; hover it
- **Your billing page** — your full account history, including anything we did
- **Support** — replying to any email from the system reaches a person

If our support team ever needs to look at your account as you, **you are emailed** — what
they were doing, when it started, and when it ends automatically.

---

## The four switches worth knowing about

| | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accounting postings** | Off until your CA has confirmed the GST treatment. Everything is recorded either way |
| **Payment postings** | The same, for subscription invoices |
| **Automatic suspension** | Whether the system may restrict access without a person deciding |
| **Provisioning mode** | Ours, not yours |

Ask before changing any of them.
