# Sales — lead to quotation

Your day, in the order it happens.

---

## The list you open first

**Solar CRM → Lead Follow-up Due.** Computed from what actually happened, not from anyone's
memory. If somebody said "call me next week", it appears next week.

---

## 1. A new enquiry

**Solar CRM → Lead → New.** Name, mobile, source, and the rough details if you have them.

The **outreach cadence** then runs itself — a four-step follow-up sequence with the
messages already written, sent from your own number. Record what happened on each call:
answered, no answer, call back, not interested. The cadence adapts.

## 2. Qualify it into a Consumer

When they are serious, open the Lead and click **Create Solar Consumer**.

The consumer record is the roof, not the person. What matters:

- **KSEB consumer number** — from their bill. Duplicate detection lives here, so the same
  roof is never quoted twice by two of your colleagues
- **Connection type and sanctioned load** — these decide what is even permitted
- **Average consumption** — from the bill, and this drives the recommended capacity
- **Bank details** — needed later for the subsidy transfer and the registration refund

## 3. Site survey

**Connections → Site Survey +.** The surveyor captures shadow-free segments with usable
area and orientation, plus structure and cable extras.

**Costing the extras at survey is the point.** Discovering them at installation is how a job
loses its margin.

## 4. Design estimate

**Connections → Solar Design Estimate +.** Capacity is recommended from consumption, roof
area and sanctioned load together — not from any one of them.

You can quote more than one option. If you do, the quotation must name which one.

## 5. Subsidy eligibility — before you quote

**Connections → Subsidy Eligibility Check +.** Eleven rules run: consumer category, prior
subsidy, capacity ceiling, DCR modules, scheme validity, connection compliance, bank
details, and more.

**Check it before you quote, not after.** Promising a subsidy the customer cannot have is
the worst conversation in this business.

Not Eligible with a good reason? Record a waiver. The quotation will not submit otherwise.

## 6. Proposal and quotation

**Connections → Solar Proposal +** for the customer-facing document, then **Quotation**.

Every solar figure on the quotation is **fetched, never typed** — capacity, subsidy,
savings, payback, statutory charges. If a number looks wrong, fix the design estimate.

**The subsidy is never a line item, a tax or a discount.** It is a government transfer to
the customer after commissioning. The app refuses a quotation that treats it otherwise, and
that refusal is protecting you.

Statutory charges — application fee, registration fee, net meter charge — print in their own
block. They are a reimbursement, not your revenue.

## 7. Order

Customer accepts → **Sales Order**. Submitting it **opens the installation automatically**,
carrying the quotation, the estimate, the eligibility check and the proposal with it.

From there it is Operations' job.

## Things that trip people up

- **The subsidy is not a discount.** It arrives in the customer's bank after commissioning.
- **Sanctioned load caps capacity.** A 5 kW connection cannot take a 10 kW plant without an
  enhancement — check at survey.
- **Duplicate consumer numbers are refused.** If it happens, someone is already on that
  roof; find them first.
- **You cannot quote from an unsubmitted estimate.** Submit the design first.
