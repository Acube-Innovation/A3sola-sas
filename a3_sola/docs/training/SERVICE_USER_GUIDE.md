# Service — the five-year obligation

A commissioned plant comes with five years of O&M. That is a real cost and a real promise,
and this is how it is tracked.

---

## The list you open first

**Solar Projects → O&M Visits Due.** Preventive visits due this week, and any that were
missed. A missed visit is a breach of your own contract before it is anything else.

---

## The O&M contract

Created automatically when a job commissions. It carries the five-year visit plan, the
service levels, and the warranty terms per component.

**Visit scheduling avoids the monsoon** — the plan is generated around it, because a
preventive visit in July gets cancelled and then forgotten.

## Preventive visits

**Solar OM Visit.** Panel cleaning, connection checks, inverter health, earthing, structure.

Record it on site. What you capture becomes the customer's service history, and it is what
you show them when they ask what they are paying for.

A visit can raise a **Service Ticket** if you find something.

## Service tickets

**Service Ticket** — anything the customer reports or you find.

**SLA by severity.** The clock runs from when the ticket is raised, and an approaching
breach escalates automatically — hourly, without anybody watching it.

| Severity | Typical |
|---|---|
| Critical | Inverter down, no generation |
| Major | Partial output loss, physical damage |
| Minor | Monitoring, cosmetic |

Tickets link back to the installation and, where relevant, to the original snag — so a
recurring defect is visibly recurring rather than looking like three unrelated calls.

## Warranty claims

**Solar Warranty Claim.** Which component, which serial, when it was installed, what the
warranty term was.

**This is where the serial register earns its keep.** You know exactly which module failed,
when it went on the roof, and whether it is in warranty — without which the manufacturer's
first question has no answer.

A replacement **updates the serial register**, so the record stays true.

## Generation readings

**Generation Reading** — periodic output against what the design promised.

Underperformance **raises a ticket automatically**. A plant quietly producing 70% of its
design output for six months is the customer's money and your reputation, and nobody
notices it by looking.

**Generation Performance** is the report that shows a slide before the customer does.

---

## Your weekly rhythm

1. **O&M Visits Due** — this week, and anything missed
2. **Service Tickets** approaching SLA
3. **Generation Performance** — anything sliding
4. **Warranty Claim Register** — anything waiting on a manufacturer
5. **Contract Renewal Pipeline** — contracts within ninety days of expiry

## Things that trip people up

- **A missed visit is a contract breach.** Record it as missed with a reason rather than
  quietly rescheduling.
- **The SLA clock runs from when the ticket is raised**, not from when you saw it.
- **A warranty claim needs the serial.** If the register was not filled in at installation,
  this is where that hurts.
- **Underperformance tickets are automatic.** If one appears, the plant genuinely is down on
  output — check before closing it.
