2.  Components Offered by Us

At the core of every solar project, we execute is our uncompromising commitment to quality. We partner with globally recognized manufacturers and utilize only best-in-class components to ensure long-term performance, safety, and reliability of our systems. Each component is carefully selected to meet stringent international standards and deliver optimal results in diverse Indian conditions ensuring your solar installation delivers optimal results for years to come.

  1. Solar Panels – RenewSys, Panasonic and Rayzon

    We integrate high-efficiency solar panels from RenewSys, Panasonic and Rayzon, three leaders in photovoltaic technology:

    • RenewSys is India’s first and only integrated manufacturer of solar PV modules, encapsulates, back sheets, and solar PV cells. Known for its robust manufacturing practices and high-quality modules, RenewSys is trusted by top EPCs for delivering consistent performance and durability, even in harsh climates.
    • Rayzon Solar is a leading Indian manufacturer of high-efficiency solar photovoltaic (PV) modules, offering a diverse range of products tailored for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. Their modules are engineered for durability, performance, and long-term reliability.

    RIM Projects being the authorized service partner for these modules, we can ensure faster service support from the manufacturer.

  2. Inverters – Solinteg, Solis and FIMER

    We use inverters from Solinteg, Solis and FIMER

    • Solinteg, a fast-growing and innovative inverter manufacturer from China. Solinteg’s products are engineered for maximum efficiency, smart grid interaction, and seamless hybrid integration. Their inverters come with advanced MPPT technology, superior heat dissipation, and intelligent monitoring capabilities, offering optimized yield and stable performance in all operating conditions.

  2. Surge Protection Device – Mersen

    To safeguard critical components from electrical surges, we rely on surge protection devices (SPDs) from Mersen, a global expert in electrical power and advanced materials. Mersen SPDs are precision-engineered for solar applications, offering superior reliability, fast response times, and high surge capacity. Their products comply with international safety standards and ensure long-term system protection.

  3. DC & AC Switches – ABB

    For switching and protection, we use DC and AC isolators and switches from ABB, a global leader in electrification technologies. ABB switches are rugged, precision-engineered, and designed to withstand high voltages and currents, ensuring safe disconnection and fault isolation. Their switches are certified to meet international standards and are widely recognized for their durability, safety, and consistent performance.

  1. DC Cables – UV-Resistant with E-Beam Crosslinking

    We utilize UV-resistant DC cables manufactured using electron beam (e-beam) crosslinking technology. E-beam crosslinking enhances the molecular structure of the insulation, resulting in superior thermal stability, mechanical strength, and long-term weather resistance. These cables are flame-retardant, halogen-free, and designed for over 25 years of outdoor exposure — a crucial factor in ensuring safe and reliable energy transmission in solar installations.
