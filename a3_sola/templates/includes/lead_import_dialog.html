Every lead added or updated goes in this campaign, unless its row names another. A new campaign is made when you import.

  1. In Google Sheets choose Share, and set General access to Anyone with the link (Viewer).
  2. Name the columns in the first row: Name, Mobile, Email, District, Loan Required… as on the lead form. To update, a Lead ID column finds each lead surely.
  3. Open the tab to import and copy the link from the address bar.
