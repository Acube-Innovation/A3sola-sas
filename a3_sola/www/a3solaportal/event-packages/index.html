Event Packages

Which package a visitor who registers at an event is offered, by their electricity bill and the answers they give on the form. The first rule that matches gives the package.

  • Rules are checked from the top. Use the arrows to change the order: put the narrow rules above the wide ones.
  • Bill from and to are for a 2-month bill. A visitor who enters a 1-month bill has it doubled first. Leave Bill to empty for no upper limit.
  • Any matches every answer. Not sure matches a visitor who left the question blank.
  • When no rule matches, the package is chosen automatically from the size the bill needs, as before. A rule's package is used even if the event lists other packages.
